ABOUT

Think. Build. Impact.

What I love about iOS development is the opportunity to see millions of people interact with the things I build every day. There is something incredibly rewarding about creating an experience that lives directly in someone's hands.

That perspective pushes me to think beyond a set of requirements and consider how a product actually feels to use — how intuitive it is, how responsive it feels and the small details that can make an experience better. I enjoy combining thoughtful engineering with a strong focus on the people using the product.
